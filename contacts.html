Custom Stone Fabrication Help

West C Tech helps homeowners, designers, builders, and contractors with custom stone fabrication and installation throughout Southern California. Contact us to discuss kitchen countertops, bathroom vanity tops, shower walls, fireplace surrounds, stone flooring, stairs, and other residential stone details.

When you reach out, include the project location, the type of stone or surface you are considering, and whether the project is for a kitchen, bathroom, fireplace, flooring, or custom interior feature. This helps us understand the measurements, material needs, edge details, slab layout, and installation requirements before the next step.

Our work includes natural stone and engineered stone surfaces such as marble, granite, quartz, quartzite, limestone, onyx, and similar materials. We can help with field measurements, fabrication planning, edge profiles, sink and cooktop openings, backsplash details, polishing, delivery, and installation. The goal is to create clean, durable stonework that fits the home and performs well in everyday use.

For faster service, send photos, rough dimensions, and any drawings or layout notes you already have. We can review the scope and help determine what information is needed before fabrication begins.

What to Include With Your Request

To help us respond accurately, please include the project address or city, the type of room, the material you are considering, and whether you already have stone selected. Helpful details include cabinet drawings, rough measurements, photos of the existing space, sink or appliance information, backsplash plans, and any edge profile preferences.

West C Tech can review stone fabrication needs for kitchens, bathrooms, fireplaces, floors, stairs, outdoor areas, and custom interior features. Clear information at the beginning helps avoid delays and makes it easier to plan templating, fabrication, polishing, delivery, and installation.

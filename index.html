Work In Process

The sequence of events which brings natural stone from the quarry to the home has not changed much since stone entered the homes of the ancient Greeks. Large blocks of stone are cut from the quarry. From those blocks, thin pieces of stone are cut called slabs. The slabs are then shaped and polished and shipped for installation.

Custom Stone Fabrication in Southern California

West C Tech provides custom stone fabrication and installation for residential kitchens, bathrooms, fireplaces, floors, stairs, and specialty stone details. The company works with natural stone and engineered stone surfaces for homeowners, designers, and contractors who need careful measuring, fabrication, edge work, and installation. Projects commonly include kitchen countertops, bathroom vanity tops, shower surrounds, fireplace surrounds, stone flooring, and custom interior stone features.
